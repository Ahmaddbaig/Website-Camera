About This Project

Sentry is a security camera built by Ahmad Baig and Usman Naveed. A Raspberry Pi controls two servo motors that pan and tilt an Adafruit camera through a 180-degree range. Users switch between manual control and Sentry Mode, which detects movement in the camera’s field of view. Recordings are saved to an SD card, and the live feed can be viewed from a phone or desktop.

Built with: Raspberry Pi, Python, Flask, OpenCV, YOLO (converted to NCNN for faster inference on the Pi), servo drivers and a small HTML, CSS and JavaScript front end.

Status: in progress. The team is still adding features and improving the hardware.

Key Features

Advanced Camera Features

Experience unparalleled control with manual mode, AI-powered tracking, and sentry mode. Enjoy seamless client-based tracking, with real-time precision on both your Raspberry Pi and the camera itself.

Live Streaming

View live camera feed with real-time updates directly from your Raspberry Pi.

Low-Light Vision

Never miss a moment, even in the dark. With low-light vision, your camera captures clear and crisp footage regardless of lighting conditions.

Laser Control

Interact with your surroundings through remote-controlled laser functions.

Multi-Device Integration

Seamlessly connect your camera with multiple devices. Access live streams, control features, and receive updates directly from your phone, tablet, or computer.

Client-Based Tracking

Enjoy enhanced precision with client-based tracking. The system ensures efficient and responsive tracking, processing data directly on your Raspberry Pi for real-time results.

Manual Control

Take control of your camera remotely. Use the intuitive interface to manually pan, tilt, and zoom, giving you complete control at your fingertips.

Smart Sentry Mode

Automatically detect and track movement in your surroundings. With AI-enhanced sentry mode, your camera stays alert and ready to respond to potential threats, ensuring maximum security.

AI Object Recognition

Your camera can identify and focus on objects of interest with AI-driven recognition. It’s smarter, faster, and always learning to adapt to your environment.
